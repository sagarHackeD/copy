\section{Introduction}

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has become one of the most significant technological
advancements of the 21st century. It has transformed industries such as healthcare,
finance, education, manufacturing, and transportation by enabling intelligent
decision-making and automation \cite{russell,norvig,mccarthy}. Machine learning,
a major branch of AI, allows computers to learn from data without being explicitly
programmed, making applications such as image recognition, speech processing,
and recommendation systems highly effective \cite{bishop,mitchell}. Deep learning,
which uses artificial neural networks with multiple hidden layers, has further
improved the accuracy of AI systems in complex tasks such as natural language
processing and autonomous driving \cite{goodfellow,lecun}.

The rapid growth of AI has also introduced challenges related to ethics, privacy,
security, and explainability. Researchers have emphasized the need for responsible
AI development to ensure fairness, transparency, and accountability in automated
systems \cite{floridi,bostrom}. In addition, advancements in data science and
cloud computing have accelerated AI research by providing access to large datasets
and computational resources \cite{han}. As AI continues to evolve, interdisciplinary
research and international collaboration remain essential for developing innovative
solutions while addressing societal concerns \cite{knuth}.
