{label:} OPCODE{cond}{S} {Rd}, {Rn}, {shifter_operand}
ADC | Add with Carry | ADC r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r1 + r2 + Carry
ADD | Addition | ADD r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r1 + r2
AND | Bitwise AND | AND r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r1 & r2
B | Unconditional Branch | B loop_start | Jump to label
B{cond} | Conditional Branch | BEQ label | Branch if Equal (Zero flag set)
BIC | Bit Clear | BIC r0, r1, #0x3 | r0 = r1 & ~0x3 (Clear bits)
BL | Branch with Link | BL my_function | Jump to subroutine (saves return address in LR)
BLX | Branch Link and Exchange | BLX r0 | Call subroutine and switch between ARM/Thumb mode
BX | Branch and Exchange | BX lr | Return from function (jump to address in LR)
CDP | Coprocessor Data Op | CDP p5, 2, c12, c10, c3, 4 | Execute coprocessor instruction
CMN | Compare Negative | CMN r0, #5 | Sets flags based on r0 + 5
CMP | Compare | CMP r0, r1 | Sets flags based on r0 - r1
EOR | Bitwise XOR | EOR r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r1 ^ r2
LDC | Load Coproc | LDC p5, c4, [r2] | Load coprocessor register from memory
LDMIA | Load Multiple (with PC) | LDMIA sp!, {r0-r3, pc} | POP multiple registers and return from function.
LDMIA | Load Multiple Increment After | LDMIA r0!, {r1-r4} | Load r1-r4 from memory at r0, then increment r0.
LDR | Load Word (32-bit) | LDR r0, [r1] | r0 = r1 (load from address in r1)
LDRB | Load Byte (8-bit) | LDRB r0, [r1] | r0 = (unsigned char)r1
LDRH | Load Halfword (16-bit) | LDRH r0, [r1] | r0 = (unsigned short)r1
LDRSB | Load Signed Byte | LDRSB r0, [r1] | Sign-extend byte to 32-bit.
LDRSH | Load Signed Halfword | LDRSH r0, [r1] | Sign-extend halfword to 32-bit.
MCR | Move to Coproc | MCR p10, 0, r0, c1, c0, 0 | Write ARM register to coprocessor
MLA | Multiply Accumulate | MLA r4, r2, r3, r1 | r4 = (r2 r3) + r1
MOV | Move | MOV r0, #10 | r0 = 10
MRC | Move from Coproc | MRC p10, 0, r0, c1, c0, 0 | Read coprocessor register into ARM register
MRS | Move PSR to Register | MRS r0, CPSR | Read CPSR into r0
MSR | Move Register to PSR | MSR CPSR_f, r0 | Write r0 to CPSR flags (N,Z,C,V)
MUL | Multiply | MUL r4, r2, r3 | r4 = r2 r3 (32-bit result)
MVN | Move NOT | MVN r1, r2 | r1 = ~r2 (Bitwise NOT)
ORN | OR NOT | ORN r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r1 | ~r2
ORR | Bitwise OR | ORR r0, r1, #0xF | r0 = r1 | 0xF
RSB | Reverse Subtract | RSB r0, r1, #10 | r0 = 10 - r1
RSC | Reverse Sub with Carry | RSC r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r2 - r1 - (1-Carry)
SBC | Subtract with Carry | SBC r0, r1, r2 | r0 = r1 - r2 - (1-Carry)
SMLAL | Signed Mult Accum Long | SMLAL r0, r1, r2, r3 | (r1,r0) += r2 r3
SMULL | Signed Multiply Long | SMULL r0, r1, r2, r3 | (r1,r0) = r2 r3 (Signed 64-bit)
STC | Store Coproc | STC p5, c4, [r2] | Store coprocessor register to memory
STMDB | Store Multiple Decrement Before | STMDB sp!, {r0-r3, lr} | PUSH multiple registers onto the stack.
STR | Store Word (32-bit) | STR r0, [r1] | r1 = r0 (store r0 to address in r1)
STRB | Store Byte (8-bit) | STRB r0, [r1] | (unsigned char)r1 = r0
STRH | Store Halfword (16-bit) | STRH r0, [r1] | (unsigned short)r1 = r0
SUB | Subtract | SUB r0, r1, #5 | r0 = r1 - 5
SWI | Software Interrupt | SWI 0x12 | Trigger OS routine number 0x12
SWP | Swap Word | SWP r0, r1, [r2] | Atomically swap r0 with memory at r2
TEQ | Test Equivalence | TEQ r0, r1 | Sets flags based on r0 ^ r1 (XOR)
TST | Test | TST r0, #0x3 | Sets flags based on r0 & 0x3 (Logical AND)
UMLAL | Unsigned Mult Accum Long | UMLAL r0, r1, r2, r3 | (r1,r0) += r2 r3
UMULL | Unsigned Multiply Long | UMULL r0, r1, r2, r3 | (r1,r0) = r2 r3 (64-bit result)
