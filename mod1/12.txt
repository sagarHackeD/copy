12.Show that [[(p↔q)Λ(q↔r)Λ(r↔p)]↔[(p→q)Λ(q→r)Λ(r→p)]] is a tautology (i.e. the two sides are logically equivalent).

Let  
[L=(p↔q)Λ(q↔r)Λ(r↔p)]
and  
[R=(p→q)Λ(q→r)Λ(r→p).]

We evaluate both for all truth assignments:

| (p) | (q) | (r) | (L) | (R) |
| T | T | T | T | T |
| T | T | F | F | F |
| T | F | T | F | F |
| T | F | F | F | F |
| F | T | T | F | F |
| F | T | F | F | F |
| F | F | T | F | F |
| F | F | F | T | T |

In every row, (L) and (R) have the same truth value. Hence  
[L↔R]
is a tautology, so they are logically equivalent.
