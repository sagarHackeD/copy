16. What is a literature review? Explain the importance of new and existing knowledge and the analysis and synthesis of prior art.

Definition: A literature review is a systematic examination of previously published research related to a particular topic or research problem.

Importance of a literature review:

1. Identifies existing knowledge and established theories.
2. Helps identify gaps and unanswered questions.
3. Prevents unnecessary duplication of research.
4. Helps select suitable research methods.
5. Provides a foundation for developing new ideas.
6. Helps compare existing solutions and identify their limitations.

Existing and new knowledge:

- Existing knowledge: Information already established through previous research, publications, and experiments.
- New knowledge: Original findings, methods, designs, or explanations developed through the current research.

Analysis of prior art: Analysis involves examining individual research papers, methods, assumptions, results, strengths, and limitations.

Synthesis of prior art: Synthesis combines findings from multiple sources to identify common patterns, differences, relationships, and research gaps.
