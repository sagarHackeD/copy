13. Explain how to find and solve a worthwhile research problem.

A worthwhile research problem is one that is important, feasible, and capable of producing useful new knowledge or solutions.

Steps to identify and solve a research problem:

1. Identify an area of interest
2. Conduct a literature review
3. Identify research gaps
4. Define the problem
5. Evaluate its importance
6. Check feasibility
7. Set objectives
8. Develop a methodology
9. Conduct research
10. Evaluate results
