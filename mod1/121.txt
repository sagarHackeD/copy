121. Symbolic form and validity

Let:
- (A): A gets the Supervisor’s position.
- (W): A works hard.
- (R): A gets a raise.
- (C): A buys a car.

Premises:
1. ((AΛ W)→R)
2. (R→C)
3. (¬C)

Conclusion:
[¬AV ¬W.]

Proof:
- From (R→C) and (¬C), by Modus Tollens, get (¬R).
- From ((AΛ W)→R) and (¬R), by Modus Tollens, get (¬(AΛ W)).
- By De Morgan’s law, (¬(AΛ W)≡ ¬AV ¬W).

Thus the argument is valid.
