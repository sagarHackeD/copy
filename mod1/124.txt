124. Definitions
(i) Proposition:  
A declarative sentence that is either true or false, but not both.
(ii) Open Statement (Predicate):  
A statement containing one or more variables; it becomes a proposition when specific values are substituted for the variables from a given domain.
(iii) Quantifiers:  
Symbols used to indicate the number of elements in the domain that satisfy a predicate.  
- Universal quantifier (▽): “for all”.  
- Existential quantifier (Ǝ): “there exists”.
