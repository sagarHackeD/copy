120. Test validity of argument
Let:
- (S): Ram studies.
- (P): Ram passes 12th.
- (B): Ram’s father gifts him a bike.
- (V): Ram plays video game.
Premises:
1. (S→P)
2. (P→B)
3. (¬V→P)
4. (¬B)
Conclusion: (V).
Proof:
- From (P→B) and (¬B), by Modus Tollens, we get (¬P).
- From (¬V→P) and (¬P), by Modus Tollens, we get (¬¬V = V).
Therefore, the argument is valid.
