15. Explain the domains of software applications.
Software applications are used in different domains depending on the problems they solve and the environments in which they operate.
Major domains of software applications
1.System software
Provides services and support for computer hardware and other software.
Examples: Operating systems, compilers, device drivers and utilities.
2.Application software
Performs specific tasks for end users.
Examples: Word processors, payroll systems, banking applications and inventory management software.
3.Engineering and scientific software
Used for scientific calculations, simulations, engineering design and data analysis.
Examples: CAD software, weather simulation and numerical analysis programs.
4.Embedded software
Operates inside electronic devices and controls their functions, often under real-time constraints.
Examples: Automotive engine controllers, washing machine controllers and medical device firmware.
5.Product-line software
Designed to serve a particular market or category of customers, with common features and configurable variations.
Examples: Accounting packages, antivirus products and photo-editing applications.
6.Web applications
Run through web browsers or web-based interfaces and often communicate with remote servers.
Examples: E-commerce websites, online banking and learning management systems.
7.Artificial intelligence software
Uses techniques such as machine learning, reasoning, search or knowledge representation to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
Examples: Chatbots, recommendation systems and image recognition.
8.Mobile applications
Designed for smartphones and tablets.
Examples: Mobile payment apps, navigation apps and fitness trackers.
Conclusion: Different software domains have different requirements for performance, reliability, security, usability and maintainability. Selecting appropriate development techniques depends on the application domain.
