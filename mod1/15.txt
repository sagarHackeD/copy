15. Validity using Rules of Inference
Premises:
[p→q,    r→s,    ¬qV ¬s]
Conclusion:
[¬(pΛ r).]
Proof by contradiction:
1. Suppose (pΛ r) is true.  
2. Then (p) and (r) are true.  
3. From (p→q) and (p), get (q) (Modus Ponens).  
4. From (r→s) and (r), get (s) (Modus Ponens).  
5. Now (q) and (s) are both true, so (¬q) and (¬s) are both false.  
6. Hence (¬qV ¬s) is false, contradicting the premise.  
7. Therefore (pΛ r) is false, i.e. (¬(pΛ r)).
The argument is valid.
