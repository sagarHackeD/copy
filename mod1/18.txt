18. Pipelining in ARM
Pipelining is a technique where the CPU works on multiple instructions simultaneously in different stages.
ARM7 3-Stages:Fetch->Decode->Execute
ARM9 5-Stages:Fetch->Decode->Execute->Memory Access->Write-Back
ARM10 6-Stages:Fetch->Issue->Decode->Execute->Memory Access->Write-Back
```
Time   |Fetch|Decod|Exec
Cycle 1|ADD|
Cycle 2|SUB|ADD|
Cycle 3|MOV|SUB|ADD|
```
