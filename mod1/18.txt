18. Prove using laws of logic:
[¬[¬(¬qΛ r)V((qΛ r)V(pΛ r))]↔r.]
[¬(¬qΛ r) ≡ qV ¬r,]
and
[(qΛ r)V(pΛ r) ≡ rΛ(qV p).]
So the inner expression becomes
[(qV ¬r)V(rΛ(qV p)).]
Using distributivity:
[(qV ¬r)V(rΛ(qV p))
≡
qV ¬rV rΛ(qV p).]
Now,
[¬rV(rΛ(qV p))
≡
(¬rV r)Λ(¬rV qV p)
≡
¬rV qV p.]
Thus the whole inner expression simplifies to
[qV ¬rV qV p ≡ pV qV ¬r.]
Therefore,
[¬(pV qV ¬r) ≡ ¬pΛ ¬qΛ r.]
So the left side is (¬pΛ ¬qΛ r), not simply (r).
Counterexample: Take (p=T,q=F,r=T).  
Then LHS = (¬TΛ ¬FΛ T = FΛ TΛ T = F), but RHS = (T).  
Hence the given equivalence is false. There is likely a typo in the problem.
