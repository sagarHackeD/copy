18. Explain effective search, technical reading, critical reading and creative reading.

 1. Effective search

Effective search is the process of finding relevant and reliable information using suitable keywords and search techniques.

Methods:

- Use specific keywords.
- Combine terms using AND, OR, and NOT.
- Use quotation marks for exact phrases.
- Apply date, author, and subject filters.
- Evaluate the credibility of sources.

 2. Technical reading

Technical reading involves understanding specialized documents such as research papers, engineering manuals, standards, and technical reports.

Features:

- Focuses on terminology, equations, diagrams, and procedures.
- Requires attention to technical details.
- Helps understand engineering concepts and methods.

 3. Critical reading

Critical reading means evaluating information rather than accepting it without question.

Features:

- Checks the validity of arguments.
- Examines assumptions and evidence.
- Identifies limitations and possible bias.
- Compares conclusions with other research.

 4. Creative reading

Creative reading involves using information from existing literature to generate new ideas and possible solutions.

Features:

- Encourages original thinking.
- Connects ideas from different fields.
- Identifies new research opportunities.
- Helps develop innovative solutions.
