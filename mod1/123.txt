123. Validity of argument
Let:
- (E): The triangle has two equal sides.
- (I): The triangle is isosceles.
- (A): The triangle has two equal angles.
Premises:
1. (E→I)
2. (I→A)
3. (¬A)
Conclusion:
[¬E.]
Proof:
- From (E→I) and (I→A), by Hypothetical Syllogism, get (E→A).
- From (E→A) and (¬A), by Modus Tollens, get (¬E).
Therefore, the argument is valid.
