14. Validity using Rules of Inference

Premises:
[p,    p→q,    sVr,    r→¬q]
Conclusion:
[sVt]

Proof:

1. (p) (Premise)  
2. (p→q) (Premise)  
3. (q) (Modus Ponens, 1,2)  
4. (r→¬q) (Premise)  
5. (¬r) (Modus Tollens, 3,4; because (q) is true, (¬q) is false, so (r) must be false)  
6. (sVr) (Premise)  
7. (s) (Disjunctive Syllogism, 5,6)  
8. (sVt) (Addition, 7)

Thus the argument is valid.
