14. What is research ethics? Explain ethics in engineering research practice.

Definition: Research ethics refers to the moral principles and professional standards that guide researchers in conducting research honestly, responsibly, and fairly.

Ethics in engineering research practice:

1. Honesty
2. Objectivity
3. Safety
4. Respect for intellectual property
5. Confidentiality
6. Environmental responsibility
7. Transparency
8. Accountability
9. Fairness
10. Compliance
