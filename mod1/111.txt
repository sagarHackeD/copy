111. Explain the impact of titles and keywords on citations. Discuss the importance of acknowledgments and attributions in research.

 A. Impact of titles on citations

1. First impression: A title gives readers the first idea about the research.
2. Search visibility
3. Relevance
4. Readability
5. Citation potential

 B. Impact of keywords on citations

1. Help search engines index research papers.
2. Improve the visibility of papers in database searches.
3. Connect papers with related research topics.
4. Help researchers find relevant publications.
5. Increase the possibility that the paper will be read and cited.

Example

 C. Importance of acknowledgments

Acknowledgments recognize people and organizations that supported the research but do not qualify as authors.

They may include:

- Funding agencies and sponsors.
- Laboratory and technical staff.
- Institutions providing equipment or facilities.
- People who offered advice or assistance.

 D. Importance of attributions

1. Give credit to original creators.
2. Prevent plagiarism and misuse of intellectual property.
3. Recognize the contributions of other researchers.
4. Support verification of research claims.
5. Respect copyright and licensing requirements.
