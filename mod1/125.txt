125. Symbolic form and negation
Statement:  
“If all triangles are right angled then no triangle is equilateral.”
Let:
- (R(x)): (x) is right angled.
- (E(x)): (x) is equilateral.
Symbolic form:
[(▽ xR(x)) →(▽ x¬E(x)).]
Negation:
[¬[(▽ xR(x)) →(▽ x¬E(x))].]
Using (¬(A→B)≡ AΛ ¬B), we get
[(▽ xR(x)) Λ ¬(▽ x¬E(x)).]
Since (¬(▽ x¬E(x)) ≡ Ǝ xE(x)), the negation is
[(▽ xR(x)) Λ (Ǝ xE(x)).]
In words: “All triangles are right angled, and some triangle is equilateral.”
