11. Define research. Explain the objectives and motivation of engineering research.

Research is a systematic and scientific process of collecting, analyzing, and interpreting information to discover new knowledge, verify existing knowledge, or solve a specific problem.

Objectives of engineering research:
1. Discovery
2. Problem-solving
3. Improvement
4. Innovation
5. Verification
6. Cost reduction

Motivation for engineering research:
- Personal interest and curiosity.
- Solving real-world problems.
- Developing new technologies.
- Improving existing products and systems.
- Industrial and societal needs.
- Academic and professional development.
