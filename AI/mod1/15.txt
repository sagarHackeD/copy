15. Define an intelligent agent. Explain the role of sensors and
actuators.
An intelligent agent is anything that perceives its environment through sensors and acts upon that
environment through actuators.
* Sensors: Collect information from the environment.
* Agent: Processes the information and selects an action.
* Actuators: Carry out the selected action.
Diagram:
Environment
Information
Sensors → Agent → Actuators
Action
Environment changes
Example: A robotic vacuum cleaner uses sensors to detect dirt and uses its motors and cleaning mechanism
to move and clean the floor.
