13. Explain the four approaches to Artificial Intelligence.
1. Acting humanly – Turing Test approach: The machine behaves like a human. Its responses should be
difficult to distinguish from a human's responses.
2. Thinking humanly – Cognitive modeling approach: The machine tries to think like a human by
studying human thinking and behavior.
3. Thinking rationally – Laws of thought approach: The machine uses logical rules to reach correct
conclusions.
4. Acting rationally – Rational agent approach: The machine chooses actions that produce the best
possible outcome.
Example: A chess-playing computer may study possible moves and choose the move most likely to help it
win.
