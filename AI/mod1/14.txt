14. Explain the Turing Test approach to AI.
The Turing Test, proposed by Alan Turing in 1950, is used to test whether a computer can demonstrate
human-like intelligence through conversation.
Working:
1. A human interrogator asks written questions.
2. Both a human and a computer provide responses.
3. If the interrogator cannot reliably distinguish the computer from the human based on their responses,
the computer passes the test.
