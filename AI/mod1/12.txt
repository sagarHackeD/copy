12. Explain any two applications of Artificial Intelligence.
1. Speech recognition
* AI systems recognize spoken words and respond to human commands.
* They can be used for booking flights, voice assistants and automated telephone services.
* Example: A person speaks to an automated system to book a flight.
2. Robotics
* AI enables robots to perform tasks with less human intervention.
* Robots can clean houses, handle hazardous materials and identify dangerous objects.
* Example: A robotic vacuum cleaner automatically cleans the floor.
Other applications mentioned in the PDF include robotic vehicles, game playing, spam filtering, logistics
planning and machine translation.
