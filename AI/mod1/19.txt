19. Explain PEAS representation for an automated taxi driver.
PEAS stands for Performance measure, Environment, Actuators and Sensors. It is used to describe the task
environment of an intelligent agent.
Component Description for an automated taxi
P – Performance
measure
Reach the correct destination, ensure safety, minimize travel time and fuel
consumption, and obey traffic rules.
E – Environment Roads, other vehicles, pedestrians, passengers, traffic signals and obstacles.
A – Actuators Steering, accelerator, brakes, display or voice output.
S – Sensors Cameras, distance sensors, speedometer, GPS, fuel sensors and microphone or
keyboard.
Conclusion: PEAS helps identify what the automated taxi must achieve, where it operates, how it acts and
what information it needs to sense.
