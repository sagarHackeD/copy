110. Explain any two properties of task environments.
A task environment can be classified using different properties. Two important properties are:
1. Fully observable vs. partially observable
* Fully observable: The agent's sensors provide all information relevant to choosing an action.
* Partially observable: The agent receives incomplete information about the environment.
Example: A chessboard is fully observable because all pieces are visible, whereas a taxi cannot know
exactly what another driver is thinking.
2. Deterministic vs. stochastic
* Deterministic: The next state is completely determined by the current state and the action performed.
* Stochastic: The outcome is uncertain and may depend on unpredictable events.
Example: The basic vacuum-cleaner world is deterministic, whereas taxi driving is stochastic because traffic
and other drivers' actions are unpredictable.
