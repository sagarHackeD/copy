25. What is the importance of Quality Function Deployment (QFD) in eliciting requirements?
Definition
Quality Function Deployment (QFD) is a systematic method used to translate the voice of the customer into technical requirements and design characteristics. It helps ensure that customer needs influence the development process.
QFD is commonly associated with a matrix called the House of Quality.
House of Quality
Customer requirements
What does the customer need?
House of Quality matrix
Customer importance, technical characteristics and relationships
Technical requirements
Measurable design and development targets
Importance of QFD in requirements elicitation
1. Captures customer needs: Helps identify and organize what customers expect from the product.
2. Prioritizes requirements: Determines which customer needs are most important.
3. Translates needs into technical requirements: Converts statements such as “the application should be fast” into measurable performance targets.
4. Improves communication: Provides a common framework for customers, developers, designers and testers.
5. Reduces misunderstandings: Makes relationships between customer expectations and technical solutions explicit.
6. Supports design decisions: Helps developers focus on technical characteristics that have the greatest impact on customer satisfaction.
7. Improves product quality: Encourages customer-oriented decisions throughout development.
8. Reduces rework: Identifies important requirements early and helps prevent missing features.
Example
For a mobile banking application:
| Customer need     | Corresponding technical requirement     |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Fast transactions | Set a response-time target              |
| Secure payments   | Authentication and encryption           |
| Easy to use       | Simple navigation and usability testing |
| Reliable service  | Availability and recovery targets       |
House of Quality components
A typical House of Quality contains:
- Customer requirements — the WHATs.
- Technical requirements — the HOWs.
- Relationship matrix between customer needs and technical characteristics.
- Importance ratings and prioritization.
- Technical comparisons and targets.
Conclusion: QFD helps translate customer expectations into prioritized, measurable technical requirements, improving communication and customer satisfaction.
