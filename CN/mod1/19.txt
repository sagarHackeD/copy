19. What is a Virtual Circuit Network (VCN)? Explain its three phases with a neat diagram.

A Virtual Circuit Network (VCN) is a packet-switched network in which a logical path is established before data transmission.

Diagram:

	Source
	   |
	Router 1
	   |
	Router 2
	   |
	Router 3
	   |
	Destination


Three phases:
1.Setup phase: A route is selected, and virtual-circuit information is stored in intermediate devices.
2.Data transfer phase: Packets travel along the established route using a virtual-circuit identifier.
3.Teardown phase: The connection is released, and associated forwarding information is removed.

adv: Consistent routing and easier packet ordering.
Dadv: Setup delay and possible interruption if the established route fails.
Exs: X.25 and Frame Relay.
