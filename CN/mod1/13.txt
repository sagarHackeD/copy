13. Define switching. Explain circuit-switched and packet-switched networks.

Switching is the process of transferring data from a source to a destination through intermediate network devices.

Circuit switching:
-Establishes a dedicated path before communication.
-Has three phases: setup, data transfer, and teardown.
-Provides predictable performance after setup.
-Resources may remain unused during silence.

Packet switching:
-Divides data into smaller packets.
-Packets share network links with other traffic.
-Does not require a dedicated physical path.
-May experience congestion and variable delays.
