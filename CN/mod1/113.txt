113. Discuss guided transmission media. Explain twisted pair, coaxial cable, and fibre optics.

Guided transmission media carry signals through physical paths.

1.Twisted-pair cable: Two insulated copper wires twisted together.
-adv: Cheap, flexible, easy to install.
-Dadv: Limited distance and susceptibility to interference.

2.Coaxial cable: Contains a central conductor, insulation, metallic shield, and outer jacket.
-adv: Good shielding and noise resistance.
-Dadv: Bulkier and less flexible.
-Uses: Cable TV and RF communication.

3.Optical fibre: Transmits data as light through a glass or plastic core.
-adv: High bandwidth, low attenuation, immunity to electromagnetic interference.
-Dadv: More complex installation and termination.
-Uses: Internet backbones and high-speed networks.
