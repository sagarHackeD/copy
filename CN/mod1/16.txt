16. Explain the four basic topologies used in networks. List adv and Dadv of each.

1. Bus topology: All devices share a single backbone cable.
-adv: Low cost, less cabling.
-Dadv: Backbone failure affects the network; troubleshooting is difficult.

2. Star topology: All devices connect to a central hub or switch.
-adv: Easy troubleshooting and expansion.
-Dadv: Central device failure can stop the network; more cabling is required.

3. Ring topology: Devices are connected in a circular arrangement.
-adv: Orderly data transmission and predictable access in token-based rings.
-Dadv: A link failure can disrupt a basic ring; maintenance is difficult.

4. Mesh topology: Devices are connected through multiple links.
-adv: High reliability and fault tolerance.
-Dadv: Expensive and complex to install.
