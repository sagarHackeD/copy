115. Explain a network and its types.

A computer network is a collection of interconnected devices that communicate and share data and resources.

Types:
1. PAN (Personal Area Network): Covers a small area around an individual. Ex: Bluetooth.
2. LAN (Local Area Network): Covers a room, office, or building. Ex: College computer lab.
3. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network): Covers a city or metropolitan region.
4. WAN (Wide Area Network): Covers large geographical areas. Ex: Internet.

adv:
-Resource sharing.
-Fast communication.
-Data sharing.
-Centralized management.

Dadv:
-Security risks.
-Installation and maintenance costs.
-Network failures can disrupt communication.
