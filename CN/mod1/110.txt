110. Write a note on encapsulation and decapsulation in the TCP/IP protocol suite.

Encapsulation is the process of adding headers and, where applicable, trailers to data as it moves down the sender's protocol layers.

Application Data
     |
TCP Header+Data
     |
IP Header+TCP Segment
     |
Frame Header+IP Packet+Trailer
     |
Transmission

Steps:
1.Application layer generates data.
2.Transport layer adds a TCP or UDP header.
3.Internet layer adds an IP header.
4.Network access layer adds frame information.
5.The frame is transmitted.

Decapsulation is the reverse process at the receiver. Each layer processes and removes its corresponding protocol information to deliver the original data to the application.
