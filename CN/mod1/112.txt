112. Explain the OSI model and its seven layers. Compare OSI and TCP/IP models.

The OSI model is a seven-layer framework used to explain network communication.

7.Application   – Network services
6.Presentation  – Data format, encryption
5.Session       – Session management
4.Transport     – End-to-end delivery
3.Network       – Routing and addressing
2.Data Link     – Framing and MAC addressing
1.Physical      – Transmission of bits

Comparison:
| OSI model--v                           
| Seven layers                          
| Reference model                       
| Session and presentation are separate 
| Physical and data link are separate   
| Developed by ISO                      

| TCP/IP model--v                           
| Four commonly taught layers              
| Practical Internet architecture          
| Included in application-layer functions  
| Combined into network access             
| Developed from DARPA/DoD networking work 

Similarities:
-Both use layered architecture.
-Both support communication between devices.
-Both include transport and network-related functions.
