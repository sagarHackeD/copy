116. Explain unguided media.

Unguided transmission media transmit electromagnetic signals through air or free space without physical cables.

Types:
1. Radio waves: Can cover wide areas and support wireless communication. Used in broadcasting and Wi-Fi.
2. Microwaves: Usually require line of sight for terrestrial links. Used in satellite communication and point-to-point links.
3. Infrared waves: Suitable for short-range communication and generally cannot pass through opaque walls. Used in TV remote controls.

adv:
-No physical cables required.
-Supports mobility.
-Easy deployment in remote areas.
-Can cover large geographical areas.

Dadv:
-Susceptible to interference.
-Obstacles and weather can affect signals.
-Security risks if transmissions are not protected.
-Performance varies with distance and environmental conditions.
