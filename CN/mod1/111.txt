111. Explain protocol layering and discuss its adv.

Protocol layering divides network communication into separate layers, each performing specific functions and providing services to the layer above it.

adv:
1.Reduces complexity.
2.Simplifies network design.
3.Supports standardization and interoperability.
4.Makes troubleshooting easier.
5.Allows independent development of layers.
6.Promotes reuse of protocols and services.

Dadv: Adds header overhead and may duplicate certain functions.
