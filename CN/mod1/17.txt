17. What are guided transmission media? Explain twisted-pair cable in detail.

Guided transmission media transmit signals through a physical path. Exs are twisted-pair cable, coaxial cable, and optical fibre.

Twisted-pair cable: It consists of two insulated copper wires twisted together to reduce electromagnetic interference and crosstalk.

Types:
1. UTP: Unshielded Twisted Pair.
2. STP: Shielded Twisted Pair.

adv:
-Low cost.
-Easy to install.
-Flexible and lightweight.
-Commonly used in Ethernet LANs.

Dadv:
-Limited transmission distance.
-Susceptible to interference, especially UTP.
-Lower long-distance performance than optical fibre.
