18. Compare OSI and TCP/IP models. What are the reasons for OSI model to fail?

| OSI model--v
| Seven layers
| Reference model
| Session and presentation are separate 
| Physical and data link are separate
| Developed by ISO

| TCP/IP model--v
| Four commonly taught layers
| Practical Internet architecture
| Included in application-layer functions
| Combined into network access
| Developed from DARPA/DoD networking work 

Reasons the OSI protocol suite did not become dominant:

1. TCP/IP was adopted early.
2. OSI protocols were complex to implement.
3. Standardization and deployment took time.
4. TCP/IP implementations were widely available.
5. Rapid Internet growth increased TCP/IP adoption.
