5. Assertion and Triggers
Assertion
A condition that must always remain true.
CREATE ASSERTION Salary_Check
CHECK (NOT EXISTS
(SELECT *
 FROM Employee
 WHERE Salary < 10000));
Trigger
Executed automatically when an event occurs.
Example:
CREATE TRIGGER Salary_Audit
AFTER UPDATE ON Employee
FOR EACH ROW
INSERT INTO Audit
VALUES (NEW.EmpID, NEW.Salary);
Whenever salary is updated, a record is inserted into Audit table.
