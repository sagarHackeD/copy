13. Why Concurrency Control is Needed?
Prevents:
Lost updates
Dirty reads
Inconsistent data
Example:
Initial balance = ₹1000
T1 withdraws ₹200.
T2 withdraws ₹300 simultaneously.
Without concurrency control:
Final Balance = ₹800
or
Final Balance = ₹700
One update may be lost.
Correct result:
Final Balance = ₹500
