2. Informal Design Guidelines for Relational Schema Design
1. Clear Semantics of Attributes
Attributes in a relation should represent facts about one entity.
2. Reduce Redundancy
Avoid duplicate storage of data.
3. Minimize Update Anomalies
Design relations to avoid insertion, deletion, and modification anomalies.
4. Avoid Excessive Null Values
Relations should not contain many null attributes.
5. Avoid Spurious Tuples
Decomposition should be lossless.
Example:
Bad Design:
EmpID	EmpName	DeptName	DeptLoc
Good Design:
Employee(EmpID, EmpName, DeptID)
Department(DeptID, DeptName, DeptLoc)
