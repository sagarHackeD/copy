6. ACID Properties
A – Atomicity
Transaction executes completely or not at all.
C – Consistency
Database remains in valid state.
I – Isolation
Concurrent transactions do not interfere.
D – Durability
Committed data remains permanently stored.
Example:
Bank transfer ₹1000 from A to B.
Atomicity -> both debit and credit occur.
Consistency -> total balance unchanged.
Isolation -> other transactions cannot see partial results.
Durability -> committed transfer survives system crash.
