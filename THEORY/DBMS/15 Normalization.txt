15. Normalization
Normalization reduces redundancy and anomalies.
First Normal Form (1NF)
No multivalued attributes.
Not 1NF:
SID	Phone
101	9876,1234
1NF:
SID	Phone
101	9876
101	1234
Second Normal Form (2NF)
Must be in 1NF and no partial dependency.
Example:
(StudentID, CourseID) -> Grade
StudentID -> StudentName
Move StudentName to separate table.
Third Normal Form (3NF)
Must be in 2NF and no transitive dependency.
Example:
EmpID -> DeptID
DeptID -> DeptName
Decompose into:
Employee(EmpID, DeptID)
Department(DeptID, DeptName)
