4. Types of Update Anomalies
Insertion Anomaly
Cannot insert department information without employee data.
Deletion Anomaly
Deleting the last employee in a department removes department information.
Modification Anomaly
Changing department location requires updates in multiple rows.
Example:
| EmpID | EmpName | Dept | Location |
| ----- | ------- | ---- | -------- |
| 1     | Ram     | IT   | Mumbai   |
| 2     | Ravi    | IT   | Mumbai   |
Changing Mumbai to Pune requires modifying multiple rows.
