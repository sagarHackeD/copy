18. Find the Pre-images
Given
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
3x-5,& x>0\\
1-3x,& x\le 0
\end{cases}
f⁻¹(0)
3x-5=0
x=\frac{5}{3}
Therefore
f^{-1}(0)=\left\{\frac{5}{3}\right\}
f⁻¹(1)
1-3x=1
x=0
Therefore
f^{-1}(1)=\{0\}
f⁻¹(-1)
3x-5=-1
x=\frac{4}{3}
Therefore
f^{-1}(-1)=\left\{\frac{4}{3}\right\}
f⁻¹(3)
1-3x=3
x=-\frac{2}{3}
Therefore
f^{-1}(3)=\left\{-\frac{2}{3}\right\}
f⁻¹(-6)
No solution.
Therefore
f^{-1}(-6)=\varnothing
f⁻¹([-6,5])
Solving
-6\le f(x)\le 5
gives
-\,\frac{4}{3}\le x\le \frac{10}{3}
Therefore
f^{-1}([-6,5])
=
\left[-\frac{4}{3},\frac{10}{3}\right]
f⁻¹((-5,5])
-\frac{4}{3}\le x<\frac{10}{3}
Therefore
f^{-1}((-5,5])
=
\left[-\frac{4}{3},\frac{10}{3}\right)
