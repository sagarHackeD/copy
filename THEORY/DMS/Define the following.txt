3. Definitions

(a) Group
A non-empty set G together with a binary operation * is called a group if:
Closure
Associativity
Identity element exists
Inverse exists for every element
Mathematically,
(G,*)
is a group.

(b) Order of a Group
The number of elements in a group is called its order.
Example:
G=\{0,1,2\}
has order 3.

(c) Abelian Group
A group is Abelian if the operation is commutative.
a*b=b*a
for all
a,b \in G

(d) Finite and Infinite Group
Finite Group
A group having finite number of elements.
Example:
Z_5=\{0,1,2,3,4\}
Infinite Group
A group having infinitely many elements.
Example:
(Z,+)
