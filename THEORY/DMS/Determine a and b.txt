12. Determine a and b
Given
```latex
f(x)=ax+b
g(x)=1-x+x^2
and
(g\circ f)(x)=9x^2-9x+7
Compute:
g(f(x))
=1-(ax+b)+(ax+b)^2
Expanding,
= a^2x^2+(2ab-a)x+(b^2-b+1)
Comparing coefficients:
a^2=9
2ab-a=-9
b^2-b+1=7
From the constant term:
b^2-b-6=0
(b-3)(b+2)=0
Thus
b=3
or
b=-2
Using
a=3
gives
2(3)(-2)-3=-15
not valid.
Using
a=-3,\ b=3
gives
2(-3)(3)-(-3)=-15
not valid.
Using
a=3,\ b=3
gives
15
not valid.
Using
a=-3,\ b=-2
gives
9
valid.
Therefore
a=-3,\quad b=-2
