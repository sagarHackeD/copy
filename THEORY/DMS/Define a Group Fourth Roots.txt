16. Define a Group and Show Fourth Roots of Unity Form an Abelian Group
The fourth roots of unity are
\{1,-1,i,-i\}
under multiplication.
Closure
Product of any two fourth roots is again a fourth root.
Associativity
Inherited from complex multiplication.
Identity
1
is the identity.
Inverses
1^{-1}=1
(-1)^{-1}=-1
i^{-1}=-i
(-i)^{-1}=i
Commutativity
Complex multiplication is commutative.
Hence
\{1,-1,i,-i\}
forms an Abelian group.
