11. State and Prove Lagrange's Theorem
Statement
If G is a finite group and H is a subgroup of G, then
|H|
divides
|G|
and
|G|=[G:H]|H|
where
[G:H]
is the index of H in G.
Proof
The left cosets of H in G partition G.
Every left coset contains exactly
|H|
elements.
Suppose there are
k
distinct left cosets.
Then the elements of G are distributed among these k disjoint cosets.
Therefore
|G|=k|H|
Since k is an integer,
|H|
divides
|G|
```.
Hence proved.
