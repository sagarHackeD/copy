17. Pigeonhole Principle
Statement
If more than n objects are placed into n boxes, then at least one box contains more than one object.
Proof of Given Problem
Total pages:
61327
Number of dictionaries:
30
Average pages:
\frac{61327}{30}=2044.23
If every dictionary had at most 2044 pages, then total pages would be
30 \times 2044 = 61320
which is less than 61327.
Therefore at least one dictionary must contain at least
2045
pages.
Hence proved.
