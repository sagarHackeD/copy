6. Solve the Recurrence Relations
(a)
Given a_n=7a_{n-1} and a_2=98
General solution:
a_n=C(7)^n
Using
a_2=98
98=C(49)
C=2
Hence
a_n=2(7)^n
(b)
Given
3a_{n+1}-4a_n=0
a_{n+1}=\frac{4}{3}a_n
and
a_1=5
Therefore
a_n=5\left(\frac{4}{3}\right)^{n-1}
(c)
Given
a_n-6a_{n-1}+9a_{n-2}=0
Characteristic equation:
r^2-6r+9=0
(r-3)^2=0
Repeated root:
r=3
General solution:
a_n=(C_1+C_2n)3^n
Using
a_0=5
C_1=5
Using
a_1=12
3(5+C_2)=12
C_2=-1
Hence
a_n=(5-n)3^n
