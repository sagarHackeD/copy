14. Show that (G,*) is an Abelian Group
Given
G=\text{all non-zero real numbers}
and
a*b=\frac{ab}{2}
Closure
Since a and b are non-zero reals,
\frac{ab}{2}
is also a non-zero real.
Hence closed.
Associativity
(a*b)*c
=\frac{ab}{2}*c
=\frac{abc}{4}
and
a*(b*c)
=a*\frac{bc}{2}
=\frac{abc}{4}
Therefore associative.
Identity
Let e be identity.
a*e=a
\frac{ae}{2}=a
e=2
Hence identity element is
2
Inverse
For a inverse of a:
a*x=2
\frac{ax}{2}=2
x=\frac{4}{a}
Hence inverse of a is
\frac{4}{a}
Commutativity
a*b=\frac{ab}{2}
=\frac{ba}{2}
=b*a
Therefore commutative.
Hence
(G,*)
is an Abelian group.
