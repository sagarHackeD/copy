51. Explain bioremediation and biomining using microbial surface adsorption.
Bioremediation is the use of living organisms (primarily microorganisms, fungi, or plants) to remove, degrade, or neutralize pollutants (heavy metals, pesticides, hydrocarbons) from contaminated environments (soil, water, sediments).
Biomining (or bioleaching) is the use of microorganisms to extract valuable metals (e.g., copper, gold, uranium) from low-grade ores or industrial waste, where traditional smelting is economically or environmentally unfeasible.
The Role of Microbial Surface Adsorption (Biosorption):
Biosorption is a rapid, passive, metabolism-independent process where microbial cell walls bind metal ions or organic contaminants to their surface. This is the critical mechanism linking both fields.
Mechanism of Surface Adsorption:
Microbial cell walls contain a variety of functional groups (e.g., carboxyl, phosphate, amino, thiol, hydroxyl) that act as binding sites for charged ions.
Textual Diagram:
```
[Metal Ion]  -->  [Cell Wall Functional Group]  -->  [Bound Complex]
 (e.g., Pb2+)      (e.g., -COO⁻, -PO_4³⁻, -NH_2)       (Adsorbed on biomass)
```
Specific Mechanisms:
- Ion Exchange: Metal ions exchange with counter-ions (e.g., Na⁺, K⁺, Ca²⁺) already bound to the cell wall.
- Complexation & Chelation: Functional groups form coordinate bonds with metal ions.
- Physical Adsorption: Van der Waals forces trap contaminants on the highly porous surface of biofilms.
- Precipitation: Cell wall components or metabolic by-products can cause metals to precipitate out of solution.
Key Microorganisms and Their Applications:
| Organism | Role in Bioremediation | Role in Biomining |
|
| Pseudomonas spp. | Degrades oil spills and adsorbs heavy metals (Cr, Pb). | - |
| Acidithiobacillus ferrooxidans | Oxidizes iron and sulfur in acidic mine drainage (passive treatment). | Primary agent – oxidizes ferrous iron to ferric iron, which leaches copper and uranium from ores. |
| Saccharomyces cerevisiae (Yeast) | Biosorbs heavy metals from industrial effluents. | Reclaims precious metals (e.g., gold, silver) from electronic waste (e-waste). |
| Bacillus subtilis | Biofilm formation for adsorbing toxic dyes and heavy metals. | Used in bio-flotation to separate minerals. |
Engineering Applications:
1.  Heavy Metal Removal: Using packed-bed bioreactors containing immobilized bacterial biomass to treat acid mine drainage and electroplating wastewater.
2.  E-Waste Recycling: Using microbial consortia to adsorb and recover gold, platinum, and rare earth elements from discarded circuit boards (urban mining).
3.  In-Situ Cleanup: Injecting biosurfactant-producing microbes into contaminated aquifers to mobilize and adsorb pollutants.
