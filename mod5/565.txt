565. Possible values of (|H|)

Given:
[K \subset H \subset G,    |G| = 660,    |K| = 66.]
By Lagrange’s Theorem, since (K ≤ H ≤ G), we have
[|K| | |H|    \text{and}    |H| | |G|.]
Thus (66 | |H| | 660). Let (|H| = 66k). Then (66k | 660 → k | 10).  
So (k Ɛ {1,2,5,10}).

Since the inclusions are proper ((K \subset H) and (H \subset G)), we have
[|H| > |K| = 66 → k \neq 1,]
and
[|H| < |G| = 660 → k \neq 10.]
Thus the possible values are:
[|H| = 66 ▪ 2 = 132    \text{or}    |H| = 66 ▪ 5 = 330.]

[\boxed{|H| Ɛ {132,330}}]
