59. Prove cube roots of unity form a group under multiplication
The cube roots of unity are
[U_3 = {1,Ω,Ω^2},]
where
[Ω = e^{2\pi i/3} = -\frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i,    Ω^2 = e^{4\pi i/3} = -\frac{1}{2} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}i,]
and (Ω^3 = 1).
- Closure: Check products:
  (1▪1=1,1▪Ω=Ω,1▪Ω^2=Ω^2,Ω▪Ω = Ω^2,Ω▪Ω^2 = Ω^3 = 1,Ω^2▪Ω^2 = Ω^4 = Ω).
  All results are in the set.
- Associativity: Inherited from ({C}).
- Identity: (1).
- Inverses: (1^{-1}=1,Ω^{-1}=Ω^2,(Ω^2)^{-1}=Ω).
Thus ((U_3, ▪)) is a group. It is abelian (commutative multiplication).
