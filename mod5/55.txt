55. Subgroup generated by (\alpha) in (S_4)

Given
[\alpha = \begin{pmatrix}1 2 3 4\\2 3 4 1\end{pmatrix} = (1234).]
The cyclic subgroup generated by (\alpha) is
[H = \langle \alpha \rangle = { e, \alpha, \alpha^2, \alpha^3 },]
where
[e = (1),    \alpha = (1234),    \alpha^2 = (13)(24),    \alpha^3 = (1432).]

Since (|S_4| = 24) and (|H| = 4), there are exactly (24/4 = 6) left cosets.  
A complete set of left coset representatives is
[{ e, (12), (13), (14), (23), (34) }.]
Thus the left cosets are:
[H,    (12)H,    (13)H,    (14)H,    (23)H,    (34)H.]
(Each product is understood as composition of permutations.)
