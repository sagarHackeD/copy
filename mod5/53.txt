53. Klein 4‑group on (A = {e, a, b, c})
Define the operation by the following table (the standard Klein 4‑group):
[\begin{array}{c|cccc}
*   e   a   b   c\\hline
e   e   a   b   c\\
a   a   e   c   b\\
b   b   c   e   a\\
c   c   b   a   e
\end{array}]
Properties:
- (e) is the identity.
- (a^2 = b^2 = c^2 = e).
- Product of any two distinct non‑identity elements gives the third: (a*b = c, a*c = b, b*c = a).
- The table is symmetric, so the group is abelian.
Thus (A = {e,a,b,c}) with this operation is a Klein 4‑group.
