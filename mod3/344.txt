344. Preimage and image computations

Given
[A={1,2,3,4,5,6},    B={6,7,8,9,10},]
and
[f={(1,7),(2,7),(3,8),(4,6),(5,9),(6,9)}.]

- (f^{-1}(6)={4}).
- (f^{-1}(9)={5,6}).
- (f^{-1}(B_1)) where (B_1={7,8}): preimage of elements 7 or 8 →({1,2,3}).
- (f^{-1}(B_2)) where (B_2={8,9,10}): preimage →({3,5,6}).
