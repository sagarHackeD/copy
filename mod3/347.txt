347. Equivalence relation on (AX A)
(A={1,2,3,4,5}),  
[(x_1,y_1)R(x_2,y_2)\Longleftrightarrow x_1+y_1=x_2+y_2.]
(i) Verification:
- Reflexive: (x+y=x+y).
- Symmetric: if sums equal, then reversed equality holds.
- Transitive: if (x_1+y_1=x_2+y_2) and (x_2+y_2=x_3+y_3), then (x_1+y_1=x_3+y_3).
Hence (R) is an equivalence relation.
(ii) Equivalence class of ((2,4)):  
Sum (2+4=6). All pairs in (AX A) with sum 6:
[[(2,4)] = {(1,5),(2,4),(3,3),(4,2),(5,1)}.]
