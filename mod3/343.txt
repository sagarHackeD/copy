343. Composition of functions on ({Z})

(f(x)=x-1,g(x)=3x,h(x)= \begin{cases}0, x\text{ even}\\1, x\text{ odd}\end{cases}).

Compute ((f∘ g∘ h)(x)):
[(f∘ g)(x)=f(g(x))=f(3x)=3x-1.]
Then
[(f∘ g∘ h)(x)=(f∘ g)(h(x))=3h(x)-1.]
Thus
[(f∘ g∘ h)(x)=
\begin{cases}
-1,  x\text{ even},\\
2,  x\text{ odd}.
\end{cases}]
Since composition is associative, (((f∘ g)∘ h)(x)) gives the same result.  
Verification of (f∘(g∘ h)):
[(g∘ h)(x)=g(h(x))=3h(x),   
f(3h(x))=3h(x)-1,]
same. Hence equality holds.
