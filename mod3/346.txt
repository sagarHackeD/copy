346. Power set and Cartesian product

Power set: the set of all subsets of a given set.

Prove (AX(B\cup C)=(AX B)\cup(AX C)):

[(x,y)Ɛ AX(B\cup C)
\iff xƐ A\text{and}yƐ B\cup C
\iff xƐ A\text{and}(yƐ B\text{or}yƐ C)]
[\iff (xƐ A\text{and}yƐ B)\text{or}(xƐ A\text{and}yƐ C)
\iff (x,y)Ɛ (AX B)\cup(AX C).]
Thus equality holds.
