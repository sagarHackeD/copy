31. Preimages of a piecewise function

Let  
[f:{R}→{R},\qquad
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
3x-5,   x>0,\\[2mm]
1-3x,   x≤ 0.
\end{cases}]

We compute the required preimages.

- (f^{-1}(0)):  
  For (x>0): (3x-5=0→ x=5/3>0) valid.  
  For (x≤0): (1-3x=0→ x=1/3>0) invalid.  
  Hence (\boxed{f^{-1}(0)={5/3}}).

- (f^{-1}(1)):  
  (x>0): (3x-5=1→ x=2>0) valid.  
  (x≤0): (1-3x=1→ x=0≤0) valid.  
  Hence (\boxed{f^{-1}(1)={0,2}}).

- (f^{-1}(-1)):  
  (x>0): (3x-5=-1→ x=4/3>0) valid.  
  (x≤0): (1-3x=-1→ x=2/3>0) invalid.  
  Hence (\boxed{f^{-1}(-1)={4/3}}).

- (f^{-1}(3)):  
  (x>0): (3x-5=3→ x=8/3>0) valid.  
  (x≤0): (1-3x=3→ x=-2/3≤0) valid.  
  Hence (\boxed{f^{-1}(3)={-2/3,8/3}}).

- (f^{-1}(-6)):  
  (x>0): (3x-5=-6→ x=-1/3\not>0).  
  (x≤0): (1-3x=-6→ x=7/3\not≤0).  
  Hence (\boxed{f^{-1}(-6)=0}).

- (f^{-1}([-6,5])):  
  We need (f(x)Ɛ[-6,5]).  
  For (x>0): (-6≤ 3x-5≤5 → -1≤3x≤10 → -1/3≤ x≤10/3).  
  Intersect with (x>0) gives ((0,10/3]).  
  For (x≤0): (-6≤1-3x≤5 → -7≤ -3x≤4 → -4/3≤ x≤ 7/3).  
  Intersect with (x≤0) gives ([-4/3,0]).  
  Union: (\boxed{f^{-1}([-6,5]) = [-4/3,10/3]}).

- (f^{-1}([-5,5])):  
  For (x>0): (-5≤3x-5≤5 → 0≤3x≤10 → 0≤ x≤10/3).  
  Intersect (x>0): ((0,10/3]).  
  For (x≤0): (-5≤1-3x≤5 → -6≤ -3x≤4 → -4/3≤ x≤2).  
  Intersect (x≤0): ([-4/3,0]).  
  Union: (\boxed{f^{-1}([-5,5]) = [-4/3,10/3]}).
