32. Composition of functions

Let (f(x)=ax+b) and (g(x)=1-x+x^2).  
Given ((g∘ f)(x)=9x^2-9x+3).

Compute:
[(g∘ f)(x)=g(f(x))=1-(ax+b)+(ax+b)^2.]
Expand:
[=1-ax-b+a^2x^2+2abx+b^2 = a^2x^2+(-a+2ab)x+(1-b+b^2).]
Equate coefficients:
[a^2=9,\qquad -a+2ab=-9,\qquad 1-b+b^2=3.]
From (a^2=9), (a=\pm3).  
From (b^2-b-2=0→ (b-2)(b+1)=0→ b=2) or (b=-1).

- If (a=3): (-3+6b=-9→ 6b=-6→ b=-1).
- If (a=-3): (-(-3)+2(-3)b=3-6b=-9→ -6b=-12→ b=2).

Thus (\boxed{(a,b)=(3,-1)\text{or}(-3,2)}).
