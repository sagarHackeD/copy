1.Identity Laws
P∧true ≡ P
P∨false ≡ P 

2. Domination Laws
P∨true ≡ true
P∧false ≡ false 

3. Idempotent Laws
P∨P≡P
P∧P≡P 

4. Double Negation Law
¬(¬P)≡P 

5. Commutative Laws
P∨Q ≡ Q∨P
P∧Q ≡Q∧P 

6. Associative Laws
(P∨Q)∨R ≡ P∨(Q∨R)
(P∧Q)∧R ≡ P∧(Q∧R) 

7. Distributive Laws
P∨(Q∧R) ≡ (P∨Q)∧(P∨R)
P∧(Q∨R) ≡ (P∧Q)∨(P∧R) 

8. De Morgan's Laws
¬(P∧Q) ≡ ¬P ∨¬Q
¬(P∨Q) ≡ ¬P ∧¬Q 

9. Absorption Laws
P∨(P∧Q) ≡P
P∧(P∨Q) ≡P 

10. Negation Laws
P∨¬P ≡true
P∧¬P ≡ false
