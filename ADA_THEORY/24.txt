
24 .Explain Greedy Criterion & Apply Greedy Method for Knapsack

1. Explain the Greedy Criterion:
The greedy criterion is a decision-making rule that chooses the locally optimal option at each stage with the hope that these local choices will lead to a globally optimal solution. 

For the Fractional Knapsack Problem, the greedy criterion is: 
"Always pick the item with the highest Value per Unit Weight (Density = V/W) first. Take as much of this item as possible before moving to the next highest density item."

Important Note: This greedy criterion only guarantees an optimal solution for the Fractional Knapsack (where you can break items). It does not guarantee an optimal solution for the 0/1 Knapsack (where you cannot break items).

2. Step-by-Step Application (Using a Standard Instance):
Since you didn't provide the specific instance, I am solving the classic textbook instance below. If your numbers are different, just follow the exact math steps.

Given Instance: 
Knapsack Capacity (`W`) = 50 kg.
Items: 
- Item 1: Weight = 10 kg, Value = Rs. 60
- Item 2: Weight = 20 kg, Value = Rs. 100
- Item 3: Weight = 30 kg, Value = Rs. 120

Step 1: Calculate the Density (Value/Weight) for all items:

Item 1: Ratio = 60 / 10 = 6 (Highest)
Item 2: Ratio = 100 / 20 = 5 (Middle)
Item 3: Ratio = 120 / 30 = 4 (Lowest)

Step 2: Sort items in descending order of Greedy Criterion (Ratio):
Sorted order: Item 1 (6) → Item 2 (5) → Item 3 (4).

Step 3: Apply Greedy Method (Fill the bag):

Take Item 1 (Weight 10): Fits completely. 
- Remaining Capacity = 50 - 10 = 40 kg.
- Accumulated Value = Rs. 60.
Take Item 2 (Weight 20): Fits completely. 
- Remaining Capacity = 40 - 20 = 20 kg.
- Accumulated Value = 60 + 100 = Rs. 160.
Take Item 3 (Weight 30): Does NOT fit completely (we only have 20 kg space left). 
- Take a fraction of it: (Remaining Capacity / Weight of Item) = 20 / 30 = 2/3 (66.67%) of the item.
- Value gained from fraction = 120  (2/3) = Rs. 80.

Step 4: Calculate Total Maximum Value:
Total Value = Value of Item 1 + Value of Item 2 + Fractional Value of Item 3
Total Value = 60 + 100 + 80 = Rs. 240.
