
25. Illustrate N-Queens problem using backtracking to solve 4-Queens problem.
Problem: Place 4 queens on a 4x4 chessboard so no two attack each other (same row, column, or diagonal).
State Space Tree (Backtracking):
- Row 1 (Col 1): Place Q at (1,1).
- Row 2: Try Col 1 (attack), Col 2 (diagonal attack), Col 3 (safe). Place Q at (2,3).
- Row 3: Try Col 1 (attack col1), Col 2 (diagonal attack 2,3), Col 3 (attack col3), Col 4 (attack diag 2,3). No safe cell! Backtrack to Row 2.
- Row 2 (Col 4): Place Q at (2,4).
- Row 3: Try Col 1 (safe). Place Q at (3,1).
- Row 4: Try Col 1 (attack), Col 2 (diag), Col 3 (safe). Place Q at (4,3).
Solution Found: Queens are at coordinates: `(1,1), (2,4), (3,1), (4,3)` – Wait, this has a conflict! Let's correct the well-known solution.
Correct Backtracking Solution for 4-Queens:
- Place Q1 at Col 1.
- Q2 at Col 3.
- Q3: No safe row. Backtrack.
- Q2 at Col 4.
- Q3 at Col 2.
- Q4 at Col 3 (only safe).
- Solution 1: `[2, 4, 1, 3]` meaning Queen in Row1-Col2, Row2-Col4, Row3-Col1, Row4-Col3.
- Solution 2: `[3, 1, 4, 2]`.
