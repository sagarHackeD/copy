
13. Design an algorithm for quick sort. Apply quick sort on these elements [25, 75, 40, 10, 20, 05, 15].
Algorithm: Quick Sort is a "partition-exchange" sort. 
1. Choose a pivot element (usually the last element).
2. Partition the array so all elements less than the pivot come before it, and all elements greater come after it. The pivot is now in its correct place.
3. Recursively apply the same to the left sub-array and right sub-array.
Step-by-Step Application (Pivot = Last element `15`):

Step 1 (Initial): `[25, 75, 40, 10, 20, 05, 15]` (Pivot=15)
- Compare with 25 (>15), 75 (>15), 40 (>15), 10 (<15 swap with 25)  `[10, 75, 40, 25, 20, 05, 15]`
- 05 (<15 swap with 75)  `[10, 05, 40, 25, 20, 75, 15]`
- Place pivot 15 in its spot (swap with 40)  `[10, 05, 15, 25, 20, 75, 40]`. Pivot 15 fixed at index 2.

Step 2 (Left sub-array `[10, 05]`): Pivot=05.
- 10 > 5, no swap. Place pivot 5 at start  `[05, 10]`. Pivot 5 fixed.

Step 3 (Right sub-array `[25, 20, 75, 40]`): Pivot=40.
- 25 (<40), 20 (<40), 75 (>40 skip), Place pivot 40  `[25, 20, 40, 75]`. Pivot 40 fixed at index 5.

Step 4 (Sub-sub array `[25, 20]`): Pivot=20.
- 25 > 20. Place pivot 20  `[20, 25]`.

Step 5 (Sub-sub array `[75]`): Base case.

Final Sorted Array: `[05, 10, 15, 20, 25, 40, 75]`.
