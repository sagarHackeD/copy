Inductive step: Assume (a_k ≤ 3^k) for all (k<n). Then
[a_n = a_{n-1}+a_{n-2}+a_{n-3}
≤ 3^{n-1}+3^{n-2}+3^{n-3}
= 3^{n-3}(1+3+9)=13▪ 3^{n-3}.]
Since (13 ≤ 27 = 3^3), we have (13▪3^{n-3} ≤ 3^n). Hence (a_n ≤ 3^n). Proved.
