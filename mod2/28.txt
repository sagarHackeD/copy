28. Explain the properties of cellulose as an effective water filter.
Cellulose, the most abundant organic polymer on Earth, has emerged as a promising material for water purification due to several key properties.
Key Properties of Cellulose for Water Filtration:
1.  Abundance and Biodegradability: Cellulose is renewable, widely available (from plants, agricultural waste), and biodegradable, making it an environmentally sustainable choice for water filters.
2.  Porosity and High Surface Area: Cellulose has a naturally porous and fibrous structure with a high surface area, providing numerous binding sites for contaminants.
3.  Rich in Functional Groups: Cellulose has abundant hydroxyl (-OH) groups that can be chemically modified to enhance its adsorption capacity for various pollutants. It can be functionalized to attract heavy metals, dyes, and organic pollutants.
4.  Hydrophilicity: Cellulose interacts well with water, which is beneficial for water permeation and filtration.
5.  Mechanical Strength: In its nanocellulose form, it exhibits enhanced mechanical properties, making it suitable for durable filtration membranes.
6.  Tunable Properties: The properties of cellulose can be tailored through chemical modifications (e.g., sulfation, esterification) and by creating composites with other materials (e.g., silk, chitosan).
Effectiveness of Cellulose-Based Filters:
| Contaminant | Effectiveness | Notes |
|
| Heavy Metals & PFAS | Highly effective | Silk-cellulose nanofibril filters have shown impressive results in removing PFAS ("forever chemicals") and heavy metals. |
| Bacteria | Effective | Cellulose membranes have been shown to retain up to 90.8% of *E. coli* and 84.5% of fecal coliforms. |
| Dyes & Organic Pollutants | High removal efficiency (80–99%) | Superhydrophilic cellulose membranes demonstrated excellent dye removal efficiency. |
| Antibiotics | Highly effective | Cellulose nanofiber (CNF) filters were highly effective in removing antibiotic solutions. |
Cellulose's combination of abundance, biodegradability, and excellent contaminant removal capabilities makes it a highly effective and sustainable material for water filtration applications.
