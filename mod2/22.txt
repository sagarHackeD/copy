22. Prove (1^2 + 3^2 + 5^2 + ▪▪▪ + (2n-1)^2 = \dfrac{n(2n-1)(2n+1)}3)
Base case ((n=1)):  
LHS (=1^2=1). RHS (=\frac{1▪1▪3}{3}=1). True.
Inductive step: Assume true for (n=k):
[1^2+3^2+▪▪▪+(2k-1)^2=\frac{k(2k-1)(2k+1)}3.]
For (n=k+1), add ((2k+1)^2):
[\frac{k(2k-1)(2k+1)}3 + (2k+1)^2.]
Factor ((2k+1)/3):
[= \frac{2k+1}{3}[k(2k-1)+3(2k+1)]
= \frac{2k+1}{3}(2k^2+5k+3)
= \frac{(2k+1)(k+1)(2k+3)}3.]
This equals (\frac{(k+1)(2k+1)(2k+3)}3), which is the formula for (n=k+1). Proved.
