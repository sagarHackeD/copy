24. Prove every integer (n≥ 24) is a sum of 5’s and 7’s
We use strong induction.
Base cases:  
(24 = 5+5+7+7),  
(25 = 5+5+5+5+5),  
(26 = 7+7+7+5),  
(27 = 5+5+5+5+7),  
(28 = 7+7+7+7).
Inductive step: Assume true for all integers from 24 up to (k≥ 28).  
For (k+1), since ((k+1)-5 ≥ 24), by the induction hypothesis ((k+1)-5) is a sum of 5’s and 7’s. Adding one more 5 gives a representation of (k+1).  
Hence proved.
