26. Binet’s formula for Fibonacci numbers
Let (Φ = \frac{1+\sqrt5}{2}), (Ψ = \frac{1-\sqrt5}{2}).  
We prove
[F_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt5}(Φ^n - Ψ^n)]
for all (n≥ 0), with (F_0=0,F_1=1).
Base:  
(n=0): (\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(1-1)=0=F_0).  
(n=1): (\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(Φ-Ψ)=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(\sqrt5)=1=F_1).
Inductive step: Assume true for all integers (≤ k) ((k≥1)).  
Since (F_{k+1}=F_k+F_{k-1}), substitute:
[F_{k+1} = \frac{1}{\sqrt5}(Φ^k-Ψ^k) + \frac{1}{\sqrt5}(Φ^{k-1}-Ψ^{k-1})
= \frac{1}{\sqrt5}[Φ^{k-1}(Φ+1) - Ψ^{k-1}(Ψ+1)].]
But (Φ+1=Φ^2) and (Ψ+1=Ψ^2) because they satisfy (x^2=x+1). Hence
[F_{k+1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt5}(Φ^{k+1}-Ψ^{k+1}).]
Proved.
