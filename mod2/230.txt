230. Recursive definitions 

(i) (a_n = 5n): same as above: (a_0=0,a_n=a_{n-1}+5).

(ii) (a_n = 3n+7): same: (a_0=7,a_n=a_{n-1}+3).

(iii) (a_n = 2(-1)^n):  
(a_0 = 2).  
For (n≥1):
[a_n - a_{n-1} = 2(-1)^n - 2(-1)^{n-1}
= 2(-1)^n + 2(-1)^n = 4(-1)^n.]
Thus (a_n = a_{n-1} + 4(-1)^n).
