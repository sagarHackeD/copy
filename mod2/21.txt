21. Prove (1^2 + 2^2 + ▪▪▪ + n^2 = \dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6})

Base case ((n=1)):  
LHS (=1^2=1). RHS (=\frac{1▪2▪3}{6}=1). True.

Inductive step: Assume true for (n=k):
[1^2+▪▪▪+k^2=\frac{k(k+1)(2k+1)}6.]
For (n=k+1):
[1^2+▪▪▪+k^2+(k+1)^2
= \frac{k(k+1)(2k+1)}6 + (k+1)^2.]
Factor ((k+1)/6):
[= \frac{k+1}{6}[k(2k+1)+6(k+1)]
= \frac{k+1}{6}(2k^2+7k+6)
= \frac{(k+1)(k+2)(2k+3)}6.]
This is exactly the formula for (n=k+1). Hence proved.
