21. Explain the different tasks which requirements engineering encompasses.
Definition
Requirements engineering (RE) is the systematic process of discovering, analyzing, documenting, validating and managing the requirements of a software system.
It ensures that developers understand what the customer needs before and during software development.
Tasks of requirements engineering
The following tasks are commonly described in the software engineering process.
1.Inception
- Identify stakeholders and understand the project's objectives.
- Establish the initial scope and business needs.
- Determine the problem the software is intended to solve.
2.Elicitation
- Gather requirements from customers, users and other stakeholders.
- Use interviews, questionnaires, observation and workshops.
- Identify functional and non-functional requirements.
3.Elaboration
- Analyze and refine the collected requirements.
- Develop models of data, functions, behavior and system interactions.
- Resolve ambiguities and improve understanding of the system.
4.Negotiation
- Resolve conflicts between stakeholders.
- Prioritize requirements based on importance, cost and feasibility.
- Agree on a realistic set of requirements.
5.Specification
- Document requirements in a structured form.
- Prepare a Software Requirements Specification (SRS).
- Describe system functionality, interfaces, constraints and quality requirements.
6.Validation
- Check whether requirements represent the customer's actual needs.
- Identify missing, ambiguous, inconsistent or infeasible requirements.
- Use reviews, inspections, prototyping and test-case analysis.
7.Requirements management
- Track requirements throughout the software life cycle.
- Control changes and maintain version history.
- Maintain traceability between requirements, design, code and tests.
