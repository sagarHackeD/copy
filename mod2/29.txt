29. Recursive definitions
(i) (a_n = 5n):  
(a_0=0), and for (n≥1), (a_n = a_{n-1}+5).
(ii) (a_n = 3n+7):  
(a_0=7), and for (n≥1), (a_n = a_{n-1}+3).
(iii) (a_n = n^2):  
(a_0=0), and for (n≥1), (a_n = a_{n-1} + (2n-1)).
(iv) (a_n = 2 - (-1)^n):  
(a_0 = 2-1=1).  
For (n≥1):
[a_n - a_{n-1} = [2-(-1)^n] - [2-(-1)^{n-1}]
= -(-1)^n + (-1)^{n-1}
= 2(-1)^{n+1}.]
So (a_n = a_{n-1} + 2(-1)^{n+1}).
