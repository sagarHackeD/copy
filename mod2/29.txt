29. employees Belagavi Problems
Given the schema:
```text
EMP(Eno, Ename, Salary, Address, Phone, DNo)
DEPT(DNo, Dname, DLoc, MgrEno)
DEPENDENT(Eno, Dep_Name, Drelation, Dage)
```
The relational algebra expressions are:
# (i) List all the employees who reside in 'Belagavi'
```text
6Address='Belagavi'(EMP)
```
or, if only employee names are required:
```text
\piEname(6Address='Belagavi'(EMP))
```
---
# (ii) List all the employees who earn salary between 30000 and 40000
```text
6Salary>=30000 A Salary<=40000(EMP)
```
or, for names only:
```text
\piEname(6Salary>=30000 A Salary<=40000(EMP))
```
---
# (iii) List all the employees who work for the 'Sales' department
```text
\piEname(6Dname='Sales'(EMP X EMP.DNo=DEPT.DNo DEPT))
```
---
# (iv) List all the employees who have at least one daughter
Join EMP and DEPENDENT on `Eno`, then select dependents whose relation is Daughter.
```text
\piEname(6Drelation='Daughter'(EMP X EMP.Eno=DEPENDENT.Eno DEPENDENT))
```
---
#(v) List the department names along with the names of the managers
Join DEPT with EMP using the manager's employee number.
```text
\piDname,Ename(DEPT X DEPT.MgrEno=EMP.Eno EMP)
```
