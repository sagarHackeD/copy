25. Prove (1▪3 + 2▪4 + ▪▪▪ + n(n+2) = \dfrac{n(n+1)(2n+7)}6)

Base (n=1): LHS (=1▪3=3). RHS (=\frac{1▪2▪9}{6}=3). True.

Inductive step: Assume for (k):
[\sum_{i=1}^k i(i+2)=\frac{k(k+1)(2k+7)}6.]
For (k+1):
[\frac{k(k+1)(2k+7)}6 + (k+1)(k+3)
= \frac{k+1}{6}[k(2k+7)+6(k+3)]
= \frac{k+1}{6}(2k^2+13k+18).]
Factor (2k^2+13k+18 = (k+2)(2k+9)). Thus we get
[\frac{(k+1)(k+2)(2k+9)}6,]
which is exactly the formula for (n=k+1). Proved.
