232. Word “TALLAHASSEE”

Letters: T(1), A(3), L(2), H(1), S(2), E(2). Total 11.

No adjacent A’s:  
First arrange the 8 non‑A letters: T, L,L, H, S,S, E,E.
[\frac{8!}{2!2!2!} = \frac{40320}{8}=5040.]
These 8 letters create 9 gaps (including ends). Choose 3 gaps for the 3 A’s:
[\binom{9}{3}=84.]
Total arrangements:
[5040 X 84 = 423360.]
