42. Solve (F_{n+2}=F_{n+1}+F_n, F_0=0, F_1=1)

Characteristic equation:
[r^2 - r - 1 = 0    →    r = \frac{1\pm \sqrt{5}}{2}.]
Thus
[F_n = A≤ft(\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^n + B≤ft(\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^n.]
Using (F_0=0,F_1=1):
[A+B=0,\qquad A\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} + B\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}=1.]
Solving gives (A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}, B = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}). Hence
[\boxed{F_n = \frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}≤ft[(\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^n - ≤ft(\frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^n]}.]
