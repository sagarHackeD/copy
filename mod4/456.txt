456. Multiples of at least one of 15, 40, 35 not exceeding 1000
Let (A=15,B=40,C=35).
[|A| = \lfloor \frac{1000}{15} \rfloor = 66,   
|B| = \lfloor \frac{1000}{40} \rfloor = 25,   
|C| = \lfloor \frac{1000}{35} \rfloor = 28.]
[|An B| = \lfloor \frac{1000}lcm(15,40)} \rfloor = \lfloor \frac{1000}{120} \rfloor = 8,]
[|An C| = \lfloor \frac{1000}lcm(15,35)} \rfloor = \lfloor \frac{1000}{105} \rfloor = 9,]
[|Bn C| = \lfloor \frac{1000}lcm(40,35)} \rfloor = \lfloor \frac{1000}{280} \rfloor = 3,]
[|An Bn C| = \lfloor \frac{1000}lcm(15,40,35)} \rfloor = \lfloor \frac{1000}{840} \rfloor = 1.]
By inclusion–exclusion:
[66+25+28 - (8+9+3) + 1 = 119 - 20 + 1 = 100.]
[\boxed{100}]
