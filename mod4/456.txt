456. Multiples of at least one of 15, 40, 35 not exceeding 1000

Let (A=15,B=40,C=35).

[|A| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{15} \right\rfloor = 66,   
|B| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{40} \right\rfloor = 25,   
|C| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{35} \right\rfloor = 28.]
[|A\cap B| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{\mathrm{lcm}(15,40)} \right\rfloor = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{120} \right\rfloor = 8,]
[|A\cap C| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{\mathrm{lcm}(15,35)} \right\rfloor = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{105} \right\rfloor = 9,]
[|B\cap C| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{\mathrm{lcm}(40,35)} \right\rfloor = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{280} \right\rfloor = 3,]
[|A\cap B\cap C| = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{\mathrm{lcm}(15,40,35)} \right\rfloor = ≤ft\lfloor \frac{1000}{840} \right\rfloor = 1.]

By inclusion–exclusion:
[66+25+28 - (8+9+3) + 1 = 119 - 20 + 1 = 100.]
[\boxed{100}]
