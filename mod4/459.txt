459. Solve (a_n = 5a_{n-1} + 6a_{n-2}, a_0=1, a_1=3)

Characteristic:
[r^2 - 5r - 6 = (r-6)(r+1)=0.]
So
[a_n = A▪ 6^n + B▪ (-1)^n.]
Initial conditions:
[a_0 = A+B = 1,\qquad a_1 = 6A - B = 3.]
Adding: (7A = 4 → A = \frac{4}{7}), and (B = \frac{3}{7}).  
Hence:
[\boxed{a_n = \frac{4}{7}6^n + \frac{3}{7}(-1)^n}.]
