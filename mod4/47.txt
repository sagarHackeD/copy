47. Positive integers (≤ 100) not divisible by 2, 3, or 5

Let (A_2, A_3, A_5) be sets of numbers divisible by 2, 3, 5 respectively.

[|A_2|=50,|A_3|=33,|A_5|=20,]
[|A_2\cap A_3|=16,|A_2\cap A_5|=10,|A_3\cap A_5|=6,]
[|A_2\cap A_3\cap A_5|=3.]
Divisible by at least one:
[50+33+20 - (16+10+6) + 3 = 103 - 32 + 3 = 74.]
Not divisible by any:
[100 - 74 = 26.]
[\boxed{26}]
