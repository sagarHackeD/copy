450. Umbrellas returned to 10 men
Let (!10) denote the number of derangements of 10 items.
(i) No man receives his own:
[\boxed{!10}]
(ii) At least one gets his own:
Total ways (= 10!). So:
[\boxed{10! - !10}]
(iii) At least two get their own:
Total (-) (none) (-) (exactly one).  
Exactly one = choose the person who gets his own (\binom{10}{1}) times derangements of the remaining 9 = (10 ▪ !9).  
Thus:
[\boxed{10! - !10 - 10▪ !9}]
