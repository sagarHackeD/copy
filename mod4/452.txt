452. Four persons to five tables (vacant chairs)

Persons (P_1,▪▪▪,P_4), tables (T_1,▪▪▪,T_5).

Restrictions:
- (P_1): not (T_1,T_2)
- (P_2): not (T_2)
- (P_3): not (T_3,T_4)
- (P_4): not (T_4,T_5)

We use the rook polynomial for the forbidden board (4 rows, 5 columns).

Forbidden cells count:
- (r_0 = 1)
- (r_1 = 7)
- (r_2 = 16) (computed by choosing two non‑attacking forbidden cells)
- (r_3 = 13)
- (r_4 = 3)
- (r_5 = 0) (only 4 rows)

Number of valid assignments (choose 4 tables out of 5 and assign persons to them):
[N = \sum_{k=0}^4 (-1)^k r_k ▪ (5-k)!
= 1▪ 120 - 7▪ 24 + 16▪ 6 - 13▪ 2 + 3▪ 1
= 120 - 168 + 96 - 26 + 3 = 25.]
[\boxed{25}]
