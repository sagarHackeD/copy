49. Integers between 1 and 300 divisible by 5, 6, or 8

Let (A_5,A_6,A_8).

[|A_5| = 60,    |A_6| = 50,    |A_8| = 37,]
[|A_5\cap A_6| = \lfloor 300/30\rfloor = 10,   
|A_5\cap A_8| = \lfloor 300/40\rfloor = 7,   
|A_6\cap A_8| = \lfloor 300/24\rfloor = 12,]
[|A_5\cap A_6\cap A_8| = \lfloor 300/120\rfloor = 2.]

(i) At least one:
[60+50+37 - (10+7+12) + 2 = 147 - 29 + 2 = 120.]
[\boxed{120}]

(ii) None:
[300 - 120 = 180.]
[\boxed{180}]
