48. Integers between 1 and 250 divisible by 3, not by 5, and not by 7

Let (A) = divisible by 3. (|A| = \lfloor 250/3\rfloor = 83).

Subtract those divisible by (3▪ 5 = 15): (\lfloor 250/15\rfloor = 16).  
Subtract those divisible by (3▪ 7 = 21): (\lfloor 250/21\rfloor = 11).  
Add back those divisible by (3▪ 5▪ 7 = 105): (\lfloor 250/105\rfloor = 2).

[N = 83 - 16 - 11 + 2 = 58.]
[\boxed{58}]
